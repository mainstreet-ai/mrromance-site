Who Is Mr. Romance

The Story

Mr. Romance (Lloyd Nance) discovered his passion for music at the age of ten, singing in church choirs and school ensembles. His talent flourished through junior high and high school, eventually leading him to perform with R&B funk bands at North Carolina A&T State University, where his smooth first tenor and falsetto earned him the nickname “Mr. Romance.”

A retired U.S. Navy Intelligence Officer with 23 years of honorable service, Lloyd entertained audiences worldwide in Department of Defense shows, performing in cities such as New Orleans, Nashville, London, Rota (Spain), Denmark, Naples (Italy), and Singapore.

Today, he brings his soulful voice to senior living communities, recently performing at Sweetwater and Everlake in Jacksonville, Florida. Married for 46 years to his wife Virginia, Lloyd is a proud father of four and grandfather to four wonderful grandchildren.
